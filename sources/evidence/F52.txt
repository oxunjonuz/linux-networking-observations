STATUS: NOT SENT. Follow-up to the F28 report; no patch is proposed.

Theme: on a VLAN-tagged frame the `@ll` base is not the frame.  With the tag in
`skb->vlan_proto`/`skb->vlan_tci`, `nft_payload_copy_vlan()` rebuilds `struct
vlan_ethhdr` and the inner ethertype sits at `@ll,16`, not `@ll,12`; once a
VLAN sub-interface exists the tag is cleared and the whole domain shifts four
bytes earlier, so the same rule reads different bytes depending on a piece of
configuration that appears nowhere in it.  And a store into the TCI is a store
into the skb, not into the frame: writing 0 there changes no byte on the wire
and turns a frame the IP stack silently drops (`PACKET_OTHERHOST`, no IPv6
counter moves) into a delivered datagram.

No diff below: this measures the LL base, the store semantics and the guard as
they stand and proposes no change.

What was measured (7.3.4-T563, one bridge with three ports, one frame seen at
five doors -- bridge prerouting, bridge forward, bridge local-in, ip6
prerouting, and netdev ingress at the far end):

* A tagged frame (vid 100) is counted by the bridge three times (`brp` 1,
  `brf` 2, `brl` 1), reaches the far end's ingress hook, and **never reaches an
  IPv6 hook**: `ip6` prerouting 0, `Ip6InReceives` 0, `Ip6InDiscards` 0.  The
  only counters that move are `rx_otherhost_dropped` on `br0` and on both
  ports, because `__netif_receive_skb_core()` sets
  `pkt_type = PACKET_OTHERHOST` when `skb_vlan_tag_get_id(skb) != 0` and
  `vlan_do_receive()` found no VLAN device (`dev.c:6207`), and
  `ip6_rcv_core()` returns on that pkt_type before `IPSTATS_MIB_IN`
  (`ip6_input.c:196` before `:206`).  The same frame with vid 0 is delivered.
* `@ll` on that frame reads the frame **with** its tag: 12..13 = `8100`, 14..15
  = the TCI, 16..17 = `86dd`, 18..19 = the first bytes of the IPv6 header --
  the 84 bytes that were injected, while the AF_PACKET taps show the 80-byte
  post-untag frame.  A rule spelling `@ll,12,2 = 86dd` cannot match a tagged
  frame; the inner ethertype is at 16.
* `@ll,14,2 = 0000` at the bridge door is accepted, writes no byte
  (`nft_payload_set_vlan()` returns before `skb_ensure_writable()`,
  `nft_payload.c:1129`), and the frame that was undeliverable is delivered.
  `@ll,14,4` is refused at evaluation time and leaves no trace: the rule's own
  counter is 0 and it logs nothing.
* `@ll,12,2 = 0806` sets `skb->vlan_proto` only.  Because `veth` advertises
  `NETIF_F_HW_VLAN_CTAG_TX`, `validate_xmit_vlan()` pushes only non-0x8100
  protos into the bytes -- that is the one arm in which the tag is a byte on the
  wire (84 bytes, `0806 0064 86dd` at 12..17).
* `@ll,12,4 = 0000 0000` zeroes both fields at once.  `skb_vlan_tag_present()`
  is `!!skb->vlan_all` (`if_vlan.h:82`) and `__vlan_hwaccel_put_tag()` stores no
  present bit, so the tag is **deleted**: the wire is 80 bytes, no byte changed,
  and the frame is delivered.
* With `br0.100` up the same frame is delivered -- and the LL domain moves four
  bytes earlier: 12 is `86dd` (not `8100`), 14 is `6000` (not the TCI), 16 is
  `0000`, 18 is the payload length.  `vlan_do_receive()` finds the device,
  `VLAN_FLAG_REORDER_HDR` (`vlan.c:276`) skips the re-insertion, the tag is
  cleared (`vlan_core.c:61`) and `nft_payload_eval()` falls back to
  `skb_mac_header() - skb->data` (`nft_payload.c:184`).
* A store into the TCI at the bridge **forward** door is evaluated once per port
  copy and reaches exactly that copy: the far end reads the new TCI while the
  local copy, which never passed that door, keeps vid 100 and stays dead.

Not measured here: 802.1ad, two tags, `VLAN_FLAG_REORDER_HDR` cleared, a VLAN
device on a port, and a physically tagged 0x8100 frame (this veth pair cannot
produce one).  Rules are installed by root in the initial user namespace, so
none of this is a privilege boundary; what it shows is that the meaning of an
`@ll` offset and the fate of a packet depend on state the rule cannot see.

-- a measurement, not a patch

# F52 -- the tag is not in the frame: a 4-byte store that changes no byte decides whether the packet lives

Kernel 7.3.4-T563 (`1efbc957…2033`), no new build. Instrument `differ574`, mode
`tag`: the F51 bridge (three veth pairs, `br0` with the ports `x1`,`y1`,`z1`,
IPv6 only on `br0`), **five doors of one frame** -- `brp` (bridge prerouting),
`brf` (bridge forward, once per port copy), `brl` (bridge local-in), `in6` (ip6
prerouting) and `ndy` (netdev ingress on the far end `y0`). Three frames, all
broadcast, IPv6 `fd02::11 -> fd02::92`, UDP 40000->5000: **A** plain, **B**
802.1Q TCI `0x0064` (vid 100), **C** 802.1Q TCI `0x0000` (a priority tag).
33 arms in each of two boots; all derived facts of the two boots are identical
line for line once the MACs the kernel picks per boot are normalised.

This closes the two holes named at the end of F51: why the local copy of a
tagged frame never reaches the ip6 door although `brl` counts it, and why
`@ll,12,2` at the bridge door is not the inner ethertype although the taps show
`86dd` at 12..13.

The mechanism is two lines that are not in the VLAN code and not in nft:

* `net/core/dev.c:6203-6211` -- after the rx handler and after
  `vlan_do_receive()` could not find a VLAN device, `if (skb_vlan_tag_get_id(skb))`
  sets `skb->pkt_type = PACKET_OTHERHOST`;
* `net/ipv6/ip6_input.c:196` -- `ip6_rcv_core()` returns on `PACKET_OTHERHOST`
  **before** `__IP6_UPD_PO_STATS(… IPSTATS_MIB_IN …)` at line 206.

So the drop is invisible to `Ip6InReceives`, `Ip6InDiscards`, `Ip6InHdrErrors`
and every other IPv6 counter; the only witnesses are `rx_otherhost_dropped` on
`br0` and on both ports, and `Ip6InReceives` stays 0. A frame with vid 0 (C)
takes the other branch -- `skb_vlan_tag_get_id() == 0`, `eth_type_vlan(skb->protocol)`
is false (the protocol is already `86dd`) -- the tag is cleared and the datagram
is delivered: `in6` 1, `u6n` 1, `Ip6InReceives` 3 (the local copy plus both
ports, where IPv6 is off and the arrival is counted and discarded),
`Ip6InDiscards` 2, and **no** `rx_otherhost_dropped` anywhere.

## 2. `@ll` on a tagged frame is the tagged frame

Every cell below is a match against the value the rule asked for, at `brp`:

## T2 -- `@ll` at the bridge door of the tagged frame (no VLAN device)
| arm | offset | len | imm | fires | ch |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| r0 | 0 | 6 | ffffffffffff | yes | 1 |
| r6 | 6 | 6 | 222dd0a3e660 | yes | 1 |
| r12t | 12 | 2 | 8100 | yes | 1 |
| r12i | 12 | 2 | 86dd | no | 0 |
| r14 | 14 | 2 | 0064 | yes | 1 |
| r16 | 16 | 2 | 86dd | yes | 1 |
| r18 | 18 | 2 | 6000 | yes | 1 |
| nh7 | 7 | 1 | 40 | yes | 1 |
| nh0 | 0 | 4 | 60000000 | yes | 1 |

`@ll` reads the frame as if the tag were present in the bytes: 12..13 =
`8100`, 14..15 = the TCI, 16..17 = `86dd`, 18..19 = the first two bytes of the
IPv6 header. Byte for byte this is the 84-byte frame that was injected -- and
not the 80-byte frame the taps show, which is the post-untag view (`packet_rcv`
pushes from `skb->data` back to `skb_mac_header`, and `skb_vlan_untag()` moved
that header by four). The mechanism is `nft_payload_copy_vlan()`
(`nft_payload.c:44`): for `offset + len > offsetof(struct ethhdr, h_proto)` it
rebuilds `struct vlan_ethhdr` with `h_vlan_proto = skb->vlan_proto`,
`h_vlan_TCI = skb_vlan_tag_get(skb)` and
`h_vlan_encapsulated_proto = skb->protocol` (`:35-37`). `@nh` is untouched by
the tag: hop limit 40 and `60000000` both fire.

So F51's contradiction is not one: the tap shows the frame after the kernel
removed the tag, `@ll` shows the frame with the tag put back from the skb. Both
are right about different layers, and the inner ethertype of a tagged frame is
at `@ll,16`, not `@ll,12`.

## 3. Four bytes that decide the fate, and one that changes nothing

One accepted store at `brp` into the tagged header, and what the wire carries:

## T4 -- stores into the four tagged bytes: what the wire carries
| arm | store | rcs | ch | u6n | br0 otherhost | y0 tap len | y0 tap 12..17 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| w14_same | @ll,14,2 = 0064 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 80 | 86dd60000000 |
| w14_0 | @ll,14,2 = 0000 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 80 | 86dd60000000 |
| w14_len4 | @ll,14,4 = 00000000 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 80 | 86dd60000000 |
| w12_p | @ll,12,2 = 0806 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 84 | 0806006486dd |
| w16 | @ll,16,2 = 0800 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 80 | 080060000000 |
| w0d | @ll,0,6 = 909192939495 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 80 | 86dd60000000 |
| w12_4 | @ll,12,4 = 00000000 | 0 | 1 | 1 | 0 | 80 | 86dd60000000 |
| wf14 | @ll,14,2 = 0000 | 0 | 2 | 0 | 1 | 80 | 86dd60000000 |
| wf14_ndy | @ll,14,2 = 0000 | 0 | 2 | 0 | 1 | 80 | 86dd60000000 |
| w14_100 | @ll,14,2 = 0064 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 80 | 86dd60000000 |

Read the table as three different kinds of store:

* **`w14_same` / `w14_0` / `w14_len4`** -- the TCI. `nft_payload_set_vlan()`
  (`nft_payload.c:789`) writes `skb->vlan_tci` and returns before any byte is
  touched (`:1129`: `if (!vlan_hlen) return;`). Storing the value the frame
  already has changes nothing (still dead); storing `0000` changes **no byte of
  the frame** -- the tap is 80 bytes, identical to the control -- and the frame
  is **delivered** (`in6` 1, `u6n` 1, `Ip6InReceives` 3, no otherhost). The
  refused length (`@ll,14,4`) never runs at all: the rule's own counter is 0 and
  the rule logs nothing, so an accepted rule that does nothing leaves no trace.
* **`w12_p`** -- the TPID. `skb->vlan_proto = 0x0806` while the TCI keeps
  `0x0064`; the frame stays dead locally, and the ports leave with **84 bytes**
  carrying `0806 0064 86dd` at 12..17. This is the first time in the project
  that a VLAN tag is a byte on the wire.
* **`w12_4`** -- four bytes at 12 set both fields at once
  (`nft_payload_set_vlan`'s `len == 4` branch, `:806-809`) to `0000 0000`. In
  this kernel `skb_vlan_tag_present()` is `!!skb->vlan_all`
  (`include/linux/if_vlan.h:82`) and `__vlan_hwaccel_put_tag()` stores the TCI
  **without** a present bit, so zeroing both fields **deletes the tag**: the
  frame is delivered and the wire is 80 bytes. A store into the L2 domain that
  removes the tag without removing a byte.

`w16` (the inner ethertype) is the byte store that crosses into the skb's data:
`@ll,16,2 = 0800` writes the physical byte at 16..17 and leaves `skb->protocol`
alone, so the local copy stays IPv6 (and stays dead from vid 100) while the wire
shows `0800` -- the protocol the stack uses is not the protocol in the bytes.
`w0d` (`@ll,0,6`) is the one store below the tag: it rewrites the real
destination MAC, the frame becomes an unknown unicast, and the local copy
disappears (`brl` 0, no br0 tap) while both ports get it.

## 4. Where the tag actually lives

The taps of `w12_p` are 84 bytes and the taps of every other arm are 80: a
0x8100 tag never becomes a byte here. `veth` advertises
`NETIF_F_HW_VLAN_CTAG_TX` (`drivers/net/veth.c:1742`, `dev->hw_features =
VETH_FEATURES` at `:1770`), so `validate_xmit_vlan()` (`net/core/dev.c:3990`)
does not call `__vlan_hwaccel_push_inside()` for `skb->vlan_proto ==
htons(ETH_P_8021Q)` and the tag crosses the pair in `skb->vlan_proto` /
`skb->vlan_tci`. Any other proto is not "HW offload capable", the tag is pushed
into the bytes, and the far end stops treating the frame as tagged at all --
which is why `w12_p` is the only arm where the ports count no otherhost.

## 5. With a VLAN device the same offset means four bytes later

The same frame, the same door, the same rule, with `br0.100` up (`vd_c`: `in6`
1, `u6n` 1, no otherhost at `br0`; the ports still count otherhost, because the
device exists only on `br0`):

## T3 -- the same offsets at the ip6 door, with br0.100 up
| arm | offset | len | imm | ch |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| vd_r16i | 16 | 2 | 86dd | 0 |
| vd_r12 | 12 | 2 | 8100 | 0 |
| vd_r12i | 12 | 2 | 86dd | 1 |
| vd_r14 | 14 | 2 | 6000 | 1 |
| vd_r16 | 16 | 2 | 0000 | 1 |
| vd_r16old | 16 | 2 | 001a | 0 |
| vd_r18 | 18 | 2 | 001a | 1 |

Offset 12 is `86dd`, not `8100`; offset 14 is `6000`, not `0064`; offset 16 is
`0000` (the flow label), not `86dd`; offset 18 is the payload length `001a`,
not `6000`. **The whole LL domain of a tagged frame is four bytes earlier once
a VLAN sub-interface exists**, and a rule that spelled `@ll,12,2 = 86dd` would
fire there and never without it.

`vlan_do_receive()` (`net/8021q/vlan_core.c:18`) finds the device, and because
`VLAN_FLAG_REORDER_HDR` is set by default (`net/8021q/vlan.c:276`) the
re-insertion branch at `:41` is skipped and the tag is cleared at `:61`; from
then on `skb_vlan_tag_present()` is false, `nft_payload_eval()` takes
`offset = skb_mac_header(skb) - skb->data` (`nft_payload.c:184`), and that
header is the 14-byte one the untag left in place. Both arms of this section
are the same bytes on the wire -- only a piece of configuration that appears
nowhere in the rule decides which of them the rule sees.

## 6. A store at the forward door reaches that copy only

## T5 -- the far end asked about the tag it received
| arm | door2 | imm | c2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| wf14_ndy | ndy | 0000 | 1 |

`wf14_ndy` stores `0000` into the TCI at `brf`; the door is evaluated twice
(`brf` 2, one per port copy), and the far end reads `0000` -- the store reached
the copy that passed that door. The local copy, which never did, keeps vid 100
and is still dead (`in6` 0, `br0` otherhost 1). A store on a clone is a store
on that clone; the copies made earlier are not reachable, which is the F51 rule
seen from the other side.

## 7. Errors of the turn, all mine

1. `pkill -f "f52_run.sh full"` matched my own shell's command line and killed
   it -- the third time in the project (F47 #6, F570 #6), and this time it
   swallowed the patch that was supposed to follow, silently.
2. My first `vd_r16b` / `vd_r18` cells carried the wrong immediate: I put the
   IPv6 payload length at bytes 2..3 of the header, where the flow label lives,
   and 4..5 is the length. A correct layout therefore looked like a hole until
   the three new cells (`vd_r14`, `vd_r16`, `vd_r18`) were spelled right -- the
   same arithmetic-against-the-header disease as F46's P10.
3. Two arms were both named `vd_r16` (one with `86dd`, one with `0000`); the
   verifier reported on the first. Renamed to `vd_r16i` and **both boots were
   rerun**, so the artifact is one binary.
4. The verifier's first model put the far end's `skb->data` at 18 for every
   arm; the 70-byte message of `w12_p` showed that a physical tag makes it 14.
5. The same model applied the store to the local copy even when it was made at
   the forward door; the `br0` otherhost counter caught it.
6. The `nfl` model counted one message too many for a forward-door store: the
   rule is evaluated twice, so it logs twice.
7. `cch()` read a bare `c2=1` as "no value", so one check passed without
   looking at anything.
8. One mutation (`x0mac`) survived because it edited the inherited F573 header
   line, which the verifier is right to ignore -- the mutation, not the check,
   was wrong.

## 8. Predictions: 18 of 20, and the two refutations are §5 and §3

P1-P15 and P17, P18, P20 held. Two did not:

* **P16 refuted.** I predicted that the tag survives as metadata through the

## 10. What is not measured, plainly

`meta vlan` / `meta protocol` on a tagged frame (the LL cells carry the tag;
the meta expressions were not used in this instrument); 802.1ad (`0x88a8`) and
two tags (`VLAN_MAX_DEPTH`, `dev.c:6217`); a VLAN device with
`VLAN_FLAG_REORDER_HDR` cleared (`vlan_dev.c:57`, settable by netlink) -- that
is the branch that would put the tag back into the bytes; a VLAN device on a
port rather than on `br0`; a physically tagged 0x8100 frame (this veth pair
cannot produce one, which is exactly why `w12_p` is the only 84-byte arm); the
store at 12 with len 4 in the direction that keeps the tag (`8100 0064`); `@th`
and `@nh` at the ip6 door of `br0.100`; GSO / `CHECKSUM_PARTIAL` on real
hardware; more than one device in one chain.

**Not a vulnerability**: the rules are installed by root in the initial user
namespace. What the numbers show is that (a) a tagged frame with any vid is
killed as `PACKET_OTHERHOST` with no IPv6 counter moving, (b) a store into the
TCI can flip that fate without changing a byte, and (c) the meaning of every
`@ll` offset on a tagged frame depends on whether a VLAN sub-interface exists.
